## Free tools & toys

- **ButterBot OVERKLOKING mod** – custom firmware for the CircuitMess ButterBot: OVERKLOKING quotes, Darth Vader / Hawking / HAL / Yoda voices, a HAL-style SHUTDOWN, plus clock, volume and night mode, and as a cherry on top of - a complete rework of the robot into a **Talkie Toaster**! This is a two parts mod, you need to flash both: [robot firmware](https://github.com/nuit-dev/ButterBot-Firmware-Public) + [controller firmware](https://github.com/nuit-dev/ButterBotCtrl-Firmware-Public)
- **[AWStatium](https://github.com/nuit-dev/awstatium)** – WordPress plugin: page views, downloads and traffic statistics from your server's AWStats data, no tracking scripts or cookies. Runs on [nuit.hr](https://nuit.hr) ([download](https://github.com/nuit-dev/awstatium/releases/latest))
